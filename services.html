Our Services

Welcome to Barangay E-Health! Here you can access essential health services online, designed to make healthcare more accessible, efficient, and inclusive for our community.

Online Consultation

Book a virtual check-up and talk to barangay health workers without leaving your home.

Health Records

Securely access your medical history and keep track of your health information.

Appointments

Schedule check-ups easily and avoid long waiting lines at the health center.

Education Hub

Learn about nutrition, hygiene, and preventive care through articles and infographics.

Emergency Alerts

Stay updated with real-time alerts on outbreaks, disasters, and health advisories.
